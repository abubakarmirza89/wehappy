# Hearteli Production Brand Kit v1.0

## Locked brand
**Hearteli**  
**Tagline:** Know when to be there.

## Logo
The included master logo is vectorised from the approved Hearteli logo supplied in the conversation. Do not alter the geometry, proportions, arm position, relationship between the two figures, or wordmark.

### Primary colours
- Hearteli Coral — `#F2645A`
- Hearteli Ink — `#1E2233`
- Hearteli Cream — `#FFF8F4`
- Hearteli Blush — `#F8E7E5`
- Hearteli Sage — `#C8DCCF`
- White — `#FFFFFF`

## Typography direction
- Primary: Manrope
- Headings: SemiBold
- Subheadings: Medium
- Body: Regular

## Core message
**Hearteli helps the people who care about you know when to be there.**

## Signature product mechanism
**Empathy Nudge™** — a timely, consent-based prompt that helps someone understand when a person they care about may need support and suggests an appropriate human response.

## Brand architecture
- Hearteli for You
- Hearteli for Families
- Hearteli for Work
- Hearteli for Therapists

## Usage rules
- Keep the approved logo intact.
- Do not redraw or reposition the embrace.
- Do not stretch, compress, rotate or recolour the master logo.
- Use the symbol-only asset when the full logo will not remain legible.
- Prefer Hearteli Cream or white backgrounds for the primary logo.
- Use the white monochrome symbol on Hearteli Ink when a reversed icon is required.
- Avoid generic heart symbols, brain imagery, medical crosses, or surveillance imagery.

## Voice
Human, warm, calm, private, useful, modern and non-clinical.

## North star
**Does this help one human be there for another?**
