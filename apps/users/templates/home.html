A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT WONDER

A little
clarity.

Who can see my check-ins?+

Your check-ins start private. Being in your Circle does not grant access to private notes or your full history. You deliberately choose what goes into each nudge.

What is an Empathy Nudge?+

A short message you review and send to a trusted person who has accepted your Circle invitation and enabled nudges. It gives them a gentle prompt to check in.

Do I have to share how I feel?+

No. You can use check-ins privately. Sharing a nudge is always your choice.

Can my manager see my personal mood?+

The workplace console does not reveal personal moods, private notes or individual check-in history. Workplace membership never grants that access.
