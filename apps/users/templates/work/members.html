About roles

Learn what each role can access and manage.

Owner
Manages members, billing and workplace settings. An active subscription is required to invite people and publish support resources.
Manager
Can view the team, workplace resources and available workplace insights. Cannot manage memberships, publish resources, change settings or access billing.
Employee
Can access workplace support resources in the app when approved and the subscription is active. Does not have access to this management console.

Workplace roles do not give access to personal check-ins or private notes.
