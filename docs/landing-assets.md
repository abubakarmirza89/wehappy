# Landing page assets

The landing page uses the existing Hearteli logo and locally served Nunito font.

`together-hero.webp` was created with built-in image generation for this project, then resized to 960 × 1440 and encoded as WebP. It is illustrative lifestyle imagery, not a customer testimonial.

Prompt: Two adult women in their late twenties, one with dark hair in a loose bun wearing a soft navy grey cardigan, the other with shoulder-length dark hair wearing a cream knit sweater, gently embracing and leaning their heads together, viewed mostly from behind and slightly side-on. Quiet rooftop garden at sunset, distant city softly blurred, warm natural peach light, authentic supportive friendship, candid editorial lifestyle photography, realistic hands and knit textures. Frame waist-up with both women together, ample soft background above them, premium calm compassionate mood. Muted coral, cream and navy surroundings. No words, no logos, no watermark, no phone, no medical setting.

The interactive check-in is a clearly marked local demonstration. It does not save or share mood data. Business calls to action use the existing Django workplace registration and login routes.
